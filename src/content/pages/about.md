Still figuring out my intentions for this site, mainly I wanted somewhere that I can keep art portfolios, projects and various ramblings outside of mainstream social media, as it seems like every day things are getting a little worse and this at least I can have some consistent expectations for.

A lot of my energy for the past few years has gone into learning about and trying to envision agricultural & community systems that can support a robust local food supply. Having lived on many small farms across the Western US, I've come to place a lot of value and importance on food, not only as a source of energy but also as the main way that we interface with and are sustained by the world. I may go into more detail on this at some point, but in short it's never been somthing that I've regretted spending time learning about.

Aside from all of that, in my free time I enjoy messing around with audio visual experiments and recently music. I fear I have too many crafts and endeavors to reasonably sustain, and there's always new things to explore... There are worse problems to have I suppose.
